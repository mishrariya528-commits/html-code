Journey Beyond Earth

Space exploration is the study and discovery of outer space. Scientists use rockets, satellites and spacecraft to explore planets, stars and other objects in the universe.

Humans have always been curious about what exists beyond Earth. Today, space technology helps us understand the universe and inspires future generations to explore new worlds.

Planets in Our Solar System

Our solar system has eight planets. Each planet is unique in its size, atmosphere and distance from the Sun.

  1. Earth

    2. Earth is our home planet and the only known planet that supports life. It has water, air and a suitable environment for living organisms.


  2. Mars

    3. Mars is known as the Red Planet because of its reddish surface. Scientists are exploring Mars to learn whether it could support life in the future.


  3. Jupiter

    4. Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system. It is a gas giant and is famous for its Great Red Spot.

Famous Space Missions

Moon Missions

  • Moon missions help scientists study the Moon.
  • Spacecraft collect information and images from space.
  • Scientists study the Moon's surface and environment.
  • Future missions may help humans explore more of space.

Mars Missions

  • Robotic spacecraft explore the surface of Mars.
  • Rovers collect photographs and scientific information.
  • Scientists study the climate and surface of Mars.
  • Future missions may send humans to Mars.

Satellite Missions

  • Satellites help in communication.
  • They provide weather information.
  • They help us study Earth from space.
  • They are also used for navigation.

Interesting Facts About Space

  • Space is incredibly large and still full of mysteries.
  • The Sun is a star at the centre of our solar system.
  • The Moon is Earth's natural satellite.
  • There are billions of stars in our galaxy.
  • Space exploration helps us understand our universe.
